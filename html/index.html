🔒 Cyber Security

Protect Your Digital World

Stay Safe Online

Cyber security helps protect computers, networks, and personal information from hackers, malware, phishing, and other online threats.

🛡 Strong Passwords

Use unique passwords with letters, numbers, and special characters. Never share them.

📧 Beware of Phishing

Don't click suspicious emails or unknown links requesting personal information.

🔄 Update Software

Keep your operating system and applications updated to fix security vulnerabilities.

Quick Safety Tips

  • ✅ Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
  • ✅ Install Antivirus Software
  • ✅ Backup Important Files
  • ✅ Avoid Public Wi-Fi for Banking
  • ✅ Keep Personal Information Private
